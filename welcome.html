Welcome to Meeting Meter

See what your meetings cost, live, while you are in them.

Try it now

  1. 1Join a Google Meet call with at least one other person. The ticker appears in the corner.
  2. 2Open an event in Google Calendar to see its estimated cost.
  3. 3Set your salary and currency in settings (default €40,000 a year).

Good to know

  • A meeting counts after 1 minute with 2 or more people. The ticker hides below 2 people by default.
  • Everything stays on this device. No account, no analytics, no servers.

Pin it: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin.
