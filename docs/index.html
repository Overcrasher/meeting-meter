Meeting Meter

See what your meetings really cost

€123.45

A free Chrome extension that turns time into money. A small ticker shows the running cost of the call you are in, and calendar events show an estimated cost before you accept them.

Add to Chrome — it's free

Free, no ads, no accounts. Works in Chrome.

What it does

Live ticker in callsWhile you are in a Google Meet call, a small draggable ticker shows "This meeting has cost €123.45", updating every second as people join and leave.
Estimate on calendar eventsOpen an event in Google Calendar and see "Estimated cost": guests × duration × hourly rate.
Weekly summaryThe popup shows this week's meetings, hours and total cost, and the most expensive meeting.
Share itOne click copies a line like "This meeting cost €187 and 42 minutes 🙃 #MeetingMeter" plus a link to install it.

Cost is based on the hourly rate you set in Options (yearly salary divided by 2000 hours). Nothing is guessed about anyone else's pay.

Works in

Google Meet and Google Calendar. Meeting Meter is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device in the browser's local extension storage. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and the extension sends nothing to any server. Meeting titles are stored only if you turn that on. Read the privacy policy, or check the source code yourself.

Install

Get Meeting Meter from the Chrome Web Store. It takes a few seconds.
